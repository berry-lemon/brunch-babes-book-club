Meet the Founder

Alexia

Alexia started the Brunch Babes Book Club with a simple belief: that the best conversations happen over good food, great books, and even better company. What began as a small gathering of friends has grown into a vibrant community of women who show up for each other — on and off the page.

She curates every monthly pick with intention, hosts each brunch with warmth, and has built a space where women feel genuinely seen. The Book Babes aren't just a club — they're a chosen family.
